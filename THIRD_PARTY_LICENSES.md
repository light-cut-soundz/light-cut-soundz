# Third-party licences

LightCutSoundz itself is released under the MIT licence (`LICENSE`).

It bundles and uses the software below. The Rust crates linked into the app are
listed, with their licences, in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.html` (generated by
`scripts/third-party-licenses.sh`). The JavaScript shipped in the window is the
app's own code plus `@tauri-apps/api` and the Tauri dialog, process and updater
plugins (MIT OR Apache-2.0).

## FFmpeg (bundled sidecar program) — LGPL 2.1 or later

The MP3, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis and M4A (AAC) exports are encoded by FFmpeg, shipped
as the separate executable `lightcutsoundz-ffmpeg` next to the app. It is built
**LGPL-only**: without `--enable-gpl` or `--enable-version3`, and without
x264 or x265. Only the pieces the exports need are compiled in (the encoders, and the JPEG and PNG readers used to copy a cover picture).

- Version: FFmpeg 7.1.1, unmodified
- Source: <https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.1.tar.xz>
  (SHA-256 `733984395e0dbbe5c046abda2dc49a5544e7e0e1e2366bba849222ae9e3a03b1`)
- Exact build recipe, with the configure flags: `scripts/build-ffmpeg.sh` in the
  app's repository, <https://github.com/light-cut-soundz/light-cut-soundz>
- Licence text: `LGPL-2.1.txt`

Because FFmpeg is a separate program that the app starts, you can replace it:
build your own with the script above (or install any `ffmpeg`), and put it in
place of `lightcutsoundz-ffmpeg`. The app falls back to an `ffmpeg` found on
the `PATH` if the bundled one is missing.
